**As built (2026-11-05), changes to interpretation 9 found during Task 8:** a `DataView` per decision day costs a full Stage 13 quality report (~51 s on the real lake), so a 2017–2022 daily history would take ~21 h. The loader therefore reads **one** view (the last decision day's) and slices it per decision: two small read-only `DataView` additions (Stage 13 module, logged in the Decisions log like Stage 15's `sessions`) — `macro_vintages(series, lookback)` (every vintage known at the cutoff; Stage 10 `known_observations` at an earlier cutoff gives exactly what that decision knew, so late first releases and revisions are handled exactly, replacing the first-release convention) and `members_on(day, name)` (membership is dated by its start/end dates). The HMM fit for month M uses the slope known at the cutoff of M's first session; the probability for `t` the slope known at `t`; `hmm.month_fit` is shared with `expanding_probabilities`. The price panel starts at a fixed anchor (`train_start` − (percentile window + 300) sessions) so rolling values are bit-identical across runs. `regime_history` = `regime_at` day by day, except (1) a split / dividend known between a day and the range end changes readings in the last bits (~1e-15 relative, labels unchanged; tested) and (2) the block list is the reading view's (real lake: EOG, HP blocked on 2020-03-16 only; breadth 0.1189 vs 0.1195). New config key `hmm.min_state_share` (0.01; degenerate EM starts). New direct dependency `threadpoolctl` (single-threaded, reproducible fits).

**Goal:** a deterministic, point-in-time market-regime read (`RegimeState`) for every decision day, with a documented `risk_scalar ∈ [0.25, 1.0]` that vol-targeting (Stage 43) multiplies into `limits.target_vol_annual`, persisted as a daily history in the lake.

**Architecture:** a new package `atlas.quant.regime`:
- `vol_trend.py` — pure, vectorised: trailing percentiles, the volatility score and label (LOW / NORMAL / HIGH / CRISIS), breadth, the trend votes and label (UP / NEUTRAL / DOWN).
- `hmm.py` — Gaussian HMM (hmmlearn) fitted on an expanding window, states ordered by volatility, forward-filtered probabilities (data ≤ t only), the monthly refit schedule.
- `macro_quadrant.py` — growth and inflation signals → Goldilocks / Reflation / Stagflation / Deflation; the credit proxy, credit z-score and the stress flag.
- `state.py` — `RegimeState` (Pydantic v2, frozen; crosses into pods / CIO / risk), the `risk_scalar` mapping, `assemble_state`.
- `loader.py` (extra file) — `DataView` → inputs → states: `regime_at(view)` (one decision day) and `regime_history(lake, start, end)` (every decision day), one code path.
- `store.py` (extra file) — lake table `regime_history` and `RegimeStore` (write / PIT read), `build_and_store`.

**Tech stack:** numpy / pandas; Stage 15 features (`vol_21d`, `price_to_sma_200`, `momentum_12_1`, through `compute_panel`) so the trend and vol inputs have one source; Stage 10 transforms (`annualized_3m_pct`, `yoy_pct`); **new runtime dependency `hmmlearn` (≥ 0.3; brings scikit-learn)** — listed in spec §11 (numerics: "hmmlearn … HMM"), so not a collision.

Interpretations and design choices (also logged in `PROGRESS.md › Decisions log`):

1. **Timing.** A `RegimeState` is dated by its **decision day** `t` (`as_of`): computed at the 08:00 decision of `t` from data known then — bars through the previous session `s = t − 1` (`data_date`), FRED market series dated ≤ `s` (available `obs + 1` day 06:00), releases whose vintage is known at the cutoff. `pit_filter` keeps rows strictly before a cutoff, so stamping a state with `t`'s cutoff would hide it from `t`'s own decision; the stored `available_at` is therefore `bar_available_at(s)` (the last input's publication, before `t`'s cutoff), which is exactly when every input of the state is known; a reader at `t` sees `t`'s state, a reader at `s` does not.
2. **Volatility regime.** Realised vol = Stage 15 `vol_21d` of the benchmark (`fund.benchmark`, SPY; annualised fraction). VIX = FRED `VIXCLS` aligned to sessions by observation date (forward-filled at most `max_fill_sessions`). Each is turned into a trailing percentile (share of the last `percentile_window` = 1,260 sessions, incl. today, at or below today; needs `percentile_min_obs` = 252). Score = max of the two (VIX missing → realised only, reported in `missing`). Label: score < `low_below` 0.25 → LOW; < `high_from` 0.75 → NORMAL; < `crisis_from` 0.95 → HIGH; else CRISIS **only if** VIX ≥ `crisis_vix_floor` (30) or realised vol ≥ `crisis_rv_floor` (0.30) — a 5-year-high in a calm window is HIGH, not a crisis (percentiles alone label ~5% of all days CRISIS: measured 4.6% on 2016–2026).
3. **Trend regime.** Three votes, each bullish / bearish / missing: benchmark `price_to_sma_200 > 1`; benchmark `momentum_12_1 > 0`; breadth = share of point-in-time universe members (`regime.universe`, sp500; membership of the decision day) whose `price_to_sma_200 > 1`, among members with a value, bullish if > `breadth_bull_above` (0.5), missing with fewer than `breadth_min_names` (20) values. Label: bullish share of the available votes ≥ `up_share` (1.0) → UP; ≤ `down_share` (0.34) → DOWN; else NEUTRAL. The benchmark MA vote is required (no benchmark → `DataError`).
4. **Credit (HY OAS) — data limit.** FRED serves ICE BofA HY OAS (`BAMLH0A0HYM2`) only from 2023-10-31 (licence; Stage 10 Backlog). So: **proxy** = duration-hedged HY excess log return `x_s = ln(HYG_s/HYG_{s−1}) − h · ln(IEF_s/IEF_{s−1})`, `h = proxy_hedge_ratio` 0.5 (≈ HYG / IEF duration), spread-widening level `L_s = −Σ x`. Measured on the lake (prototype, worklog): z > 2 on 0% → max 7.4 in Feb–Apr 2020, 19.5% of 2022 sessions, 4.4% of 2016–19 (unhedged h = 0 flags 36% of 2022 because both legs fell; h = 1 misses 2022). **Stress z-score** = (level − trailing mean) / trailing std over `zscore_window` 252 observations: `source: auto` uses the OAS level when at least `zscore_window` OAS observations are known at `t`, else the proxy (the source is in `RegimeState.credit_source`). **HMM credit feature** = the proxy's daily change `−x_s` always (an expanding window from 2015 can never be covered by a 3-year OAS series; mixing two units inside one fit would break it). The brief's "HY OAS change" is read as "credit-spread change, measured by the proxy" — recorded.
5. **Statistical regime (HMM).** Features per session `s`: benchmark simple return, `vol_21d`, credit-proxy change (above), curve slope `T10Y2Y` (pp, forward-filled ≤ `max_fill_sessions`). Rows with any missing feature are dropped. `GaussianHMM(n_components = n_states (3), covariance_type full)`, standardised with the training window's mean / std (stored in the fit), `n_init` (5) starts seeded `seed + i`, best log-likelihood kept. **Expanding window, refit monthly, PIT:** the fit used in calendar month M is fitted on the feature rows of sessions **before M's first session**, starting at `train_start` (2015-01-01); before `min_obs` (504) rows there is no fit and `hmm_probs` is `None`. Probabilities at `t` = forward-**filtered** `P(state_s | rows ≤ s)` of the fit for `t`'s month (never smoothed). **States ordered by volatility**: ascending mean of the `vol` feature in original units; labels `calm` / `turbulent` (2) or `calm` / `normal` / `turbulent` (3). `hmm_probs` is the tuple in that order.
6. **Macro quadrant.** Growth votes (each +1 / −1 / missing): `INDPRO` and `PAYEMS` 3-month annualised % minus YoY % (> 0 = accelerating); `ICSA` mean of the last `claims_short_weeks` (4) minus mean of the last `claims_long_weeks` (26) weekly values (< 0 = growth up). Growth up ⇔ mean vote > 0 (a tie counts as down: fail safe). Inflation: `CPILFESL` 3-month annualised % minus YoY % ≥ 0 → rising (tie → rising: fail safe). Quadrant: growth up & inflation falling → Goldilocks; up & rising → Reflation; down & rising → Stagflation; down & falling → Deflation. No core CPI or no growth vote → `None` (in `missing`). Values come from `DataView.macro` (the vintage known at `t`).
7. **Stress flag** = credit z > `zscore_threshold` (2.0) **or** latest known `NFCI` > `nfci_threshold` (0.0). A missing input is left out (and listed in `missing`); both missing → `False` with both listed.
8. **risk_scalar** (deterministic, documented table in `config/fund.yaml › quant.regime.risk_scalar`): product of multipliers — vol {LOW 1.0, NORMAL 1.0, HIGH 0.7, CRISIS 0.4} × trend {UP 1.0, NEUTRAL 0.9, DOWN 0.75} × stress (0.6 if flagged) × HMM (0.85 if P(turbulent) ≥ `hmm.turbulent_prob` 0.5) × quadrant {Goldilocks 1.0, Reflation 1.0, Stagflation 0.85, Deflation 0.9}; a missing component multiplies by 1; clipped to [`floor` 0.25, `cap` 1.0]. Config validators: every multiplier in (0, 1], vol and trend tables monotone (a worse label never raises the scalar), 0.25 ≤ floor ≤ cap ≤ 1. Stage 43 multiplies `limits.target_vol_annual` by it (not built here).
9. **One code path, PIT data.** `regime_at(view)` and `regime_history(lake, start, end)` share `_compute`. Macro: one `DataView.macro` read per decision day (`view.advance(t)`; vintages known at `t`); daily market series enter with the value **first** known (each decision appends the observation dates it newly sees; later revisions are not applied to past rows — first-release convention). Prices: one panel read at the last decision day's view (as Stage 19 `load_cross_sections`); every price input is a ratio of adjusted closes inside a trailing window (`vol_21d`, `price_to_sma_200`, `momentum_12_1`, returns), which a later split or dividend back-adjustment multiplies through unchanged — tested: history = per-day `regime_at` on a world with future rows and a later split. Membership: `view.advance(t).universe()` per decision day.
10. **Persistence.** Lake table `regime_history` (key `date` = decision day; columns: `data_date`, labels, `stress_flag`, `risk_scalar`, `hmm_p0..hmm_p2` nullable, `hmm_states`, `credit_source`, the numeric readings, `missing` (string list), `config_hash`, `available_at` = `bar_available_at(data_date)`). `RegimeStore(lake, config)` writes (replacing the same dates), reads history and the latest state known at an as-of (`pit_filter` + `assert_no_lookahead`), only rows of the current `config_hash`. The store lake may differ from the data lake (the main checkout's lake is read-only from the sandbox).
11. **Config** `cfg.quant.regime` (`RegimeConfig`, YAML `config/fund.yaml › quant.regime`): `universe`, `train_start`, `max_fill_sessions`, and the sub-sections `vol`, `trend`, `hmm`, `credit`, `stress`, `macro`, `risk_scalar` with the values above.
12. **"Flagged" on real data (acceptance).** Defined before the run: **2020 episode** (decision days 2020-03-09 … 2020-04-09): vol regime CRISIS on ≥ 50% of the days, stress flag on ≥ 50%, `risk_scalar` ≤ 0.5 on ≥ 50%, HMM P(turbulent) ≥ 0.5 on ≥ 50%. **2022 episode** (2022-01-03 … 2022-10-31): trend DOWN on ≥ 50% of the days, vol HIGH or CRISIS on ≥ 40%, `risk_scalar` < 1 on ≥ 80%. **Calm control** (2017-01-03 … 2017-12-29): `risk_scalar` < 1 on ≤ 25% of the days and no CRISIS day. Numbers come from the real lake (main checkout, read only) and are recorded.

## Design decisions
- **D1 Errors.** Bad arguments → `ValueError`; missing benchmark data or too short a window → `atlas.core.errors.DataError`; optional components missing → `None` / reported in `RegimeState.missing`, never guessed.
- **D2 Types.** `RegimeState` Pydantic v2 (frozen, `extra="forbid"`, `Literal` labels, `risk_scalar` constrained to [0.25, 1]); `HMMFit` a frozen dataclass with read-only arrays.
- **D3 Randomness.** `fit_hmm` takes `seed`; the history uses `quant.regime.hmm.seed` (21); two runs give identical states (tested).
- **D4 Logging.** structlog `get_logger(__name__)`: refits (`regime.hmm_refit`), history progress, store writes.
